# Apply for and Review Airdrops

The current PWA has no general airdrop application or review module. Airdrop and referral calculations in historical DAO channel contracts belong to specific contract state, not to a network-wide eligibility system.

Any future campaign must publish its network, contract, asset, eligibility snapshot, claim/refund rules, and verification entry. This page intentionally provides no claim link until a campaign is registered.
