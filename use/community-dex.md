# Join a Community DEX

The current reference L2 markets are [satsnet.ordx.market](https://satsnet.ordx.market/swap/?network=mainnet) for mainnet and [test-satsnet.ordx.market](https://test-satsnet.ordx.market/swap/?network=testnet) for testnet. No community-DEX registry or white-label certification mechanism has been released.

Verify the domain, wallet-approval origin, network, contracts, assets, and fees before using a third-party DEX. A site must not request a mnemonic or bypass PWA approval through an iframe. See [Your First Swap](first-swap.md).
